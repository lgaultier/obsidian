## Professional Experience

  

**2014–2015 (2 years)**  

**Postdoctoral Researcher — JPL (Caltech / NASA), Pasadena, USA**

- Processing, statistical analysis, and reconstruction of satellite data (temperature, altimetry) at meso- and submesoscales  

- Research on the exploitation of future satellite products in the context of the SWOT mission  

  

**2010–2013**  

**PhD — LEGI / LGGE, Grenoble, France (CNRS–CNES funded)**
