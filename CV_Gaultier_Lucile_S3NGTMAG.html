Scientific Interest

Remote sensing observation of upper ocean dynamics using multi-sensor data synergy.

Development of scientific simulator for future oceanic satellite mission and production of synthetic satellite data

\My research focuses on the multi-sensor observation of upper ocean dynamics, specifically the transition from mesoscale to submesoscale processes. I specialize in the development of scientific simulators and advanced data assimilation (Lagrangian diagnostics, image assimilation) to reconstruct high-resolution 2D oceanic fields. My goal is to bridge the gap between future wide-swath altimetry missions (like SWOT and S3NG-T) and operational ocean forecasting requirements.
