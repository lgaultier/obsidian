Label: Drifters 15m drogue hourly (AOML, NOAA)

This product represents the trajectory of drifting buoys drogued at 15m and that are part of the GDP (Global Drifter Program). 
The color represent the velocity in m/s.
The raw trajectory has been filtered and interpolated at 1h following the Algorithm described in Elipot et al (2016)

* This product is processed in DT from 2010-01-01 to 2022-10-31
* Institution: AOML, NOAA

The dataset can be downloaded at
https://www.ncei.noaa.gov/access/metadata/landing-page/bin/iso?id=gov.noaa.nodc:AOML-GDP-1hr
