We delivered and installed the two ADCP systems that's on the vessel, the one malfunctioning is an RDI 75 kHz system.

  

As you mention there is also a new Kongsberg ADCP on the vessel, is a 270-450 kHz with extreme vertical resolution, 

But obviously with lower range performance compared to the 75 kHz.

- There are some other advantages like much higher instantaneous ping accuracy which means the data will be better in rough weather or very dynamic environment, so I'm very keen to see what the community here can get out of the data.
    
- Maybe worth taking a look at this website, a very clever WHOI professor has helped the Icelandic fishery institute set up and manage their combined "Kongsberg/RDI" vessel setup: [Thorunn SADCP](https://scienceweb.whoi.edu/seasoar/adcp/web/)
    

- They're using the 150, but you get an idea of the resolution and increased information in these data.
    
- I know SHOM in France recently hired a phd specifically to work on vertical currents data which they had never seen before in their ship mounted ADCP data!
